# Chrome Web Store 0.6.14 Screenshot Storyboard

Canvas: 1280 × 800. Every image must remain understandable at 640 × 400.

Locales: `en`, `ru`, `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`. English is also the global fallback.

## 01 — Area to GIF

Product scene: the 0.6.14 recording entry and a visible area-selection frame on a normal web page.

English headline: `Record a page area as GIF`

English subtitle: `Select the important part and open it directly in Studio`

Proof required: `Record GIF`, area selection, and the selected region must be recognizable. Do not imply element capture or support on restricted Chrome pages.

## 02 — Studio workflow

Product scene: Studio with a real recording loaded and the responsive timeline visible.

English headline: `Polish every product moment`

English subtitle: `Trim, crop, zoom, and style on one responsive timeline`

Proof required: the current 0.6.14 Studio structure, timeline, and editing controls. Avoid tiny labels that cannot be read at store-card size.

## 03 — GIF export

Product scene: the GIF export settings with width, frame rate, finite loops, and size guidance visible.

English headline: `Tune GIFs for email`

English subtitle: `Choose width, frame rate, finite loops, and review size guidance`

Proof required: real settings from 0.6.14. Do not promise a guaranteed 1 MB file.

## 04 — Video export

Product scene: the video export panel with MP4 and WebM options.

English headline: `Export the format that fits`

English subtitle: `Save polished MP4, WebM, or GIF files locally`

Proof required: MP4 and WebM must be visible in the current UI. Do not imply cloud delivery.

## 05 — Local recording library

Product scene: the current Recording Manager with real local recordings.

English headline: `Your recordings stay on your device`

English subtitle: `Find, reopen, and manage every local take`

Proof required: the current local library. Do not imply cloud backup or cross-device sync.

## Visual direction

- Lead with the product UI; supporting graphics must not overpower it.
- Use one restrained accent system across all five images and high contrast for text.
- Keep headline and subtitle in the same safe area across the set.
- Prefer one clear product proof per image instead of a collage of small details.
- Use square, current UI captures with no stale version numbers, cursor artifacts, personal data, or browser notifications.
- Review every localized version for text wrapping, clipping, and terminology before export.
