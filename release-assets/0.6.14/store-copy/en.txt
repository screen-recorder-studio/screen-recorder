Turn a focused product flow into a polished GIF or video—without uploading your recording or opening a separate editor.

RECORD THE RIGHT THING

• Select an area on a supported web page and record it for a focused GIF workflow
• Or capture a browser tab, application window, or your entire screen
• Start the countdown only after choosing the source, keeping the system picker out of the recording

POLISH IT IN STUDIO

Every completed recording opens in the built-in Studio, where you can:

• Trim the beginning and end
• Crop the frame precisely
• Add smooth zooms and focus points
• Adjust the background, padding, rounded corners, and shadow
• Preview the finished composition before export

EXPORT FOR EMAIL, WEB, OR SHARING

• GIF: email-oriented width, frame-rate, and finite-loop defaults, with estimated-size guidance before export
• MP4 (H.264): broad playback and sharing compatibility
• WebM: efficient video for the web

Short, focused flows work best for GIF. For longer or high-motion recordings, Studio helps you trim the clip or choose lighter output settings.

LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY

Recording, editing, and export run locally in Chrome. Recordings are stored in the browser's Origin Private File System and can be reopened from the built-in recording manager. Captured media is not uploaded to a server as part of the recording, editing, or export workflow.

MADE FOR CLEAR PRODUCT STORIES

• SaaS feature launches and product demos
• Support videos and bug reports
• Technical documentation and onboarding
• Design prototype walkthroughs
• Short tutorials and training clips

Screen Recorder Studio is MIT licensed and open source. Capture the important part, polish it in Studio, and export a standard file without leaving Chrome.
